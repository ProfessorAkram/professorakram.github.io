# Akram Taghavi-Burris

**Program Director and Instructional Associate Professor of Computer Simulation & Gaming**  
**Tandy School of Computer Science · The University of Tulsa**

I am an educator, designer, and technologist working at the intersection of computer science, interactive media, game design, and immersive simulation. As Program Director of Computer Simulation & Gaming (CSG) at The University of Tulsa, I develop interdisciplinary learning experiences that bring together programming, systems design, creative problem-solving, and emerging technologies.

My work focuses on helping students understand not only how to build interactive experiences, but also how to design the underlying systems that make them responsive, engaging, and meaningful. Through project-based instruction, modular software architecture, and applied creative practice, I connect technical foundations with the imaginative possibilities of interactive media.

## Teaching & Academic Interests

My teaching and curriculum development span several interconnected areas:

- **Game Design & Development:** Game mechanics, interactive systems, software architecture, and player experience.
- **Modular Programming & Systems Design:** Component-based development, reusable software systems, design patterns, and maintainable interactive applications.
- **Extended Reality & Immersive Technologies:** Virtual and augmented reality, interactive environments, and experiential learning.
- **Simulation & Digital Twins:** Real-time simulation, virtual representations of physical systems, and interactive applications for research, training, and exploration.
- **Applied AI & Interactive Systems:** Exploring how artificial intelligence and emerging technologies can support adaptive experiences, simulation, and human-computer interaction.

I approach computer simulation and gaming as an interdisciplinary field where computer science, art, design, storytelling, and human interaction inform one another. My goal is to help students develop both the technical expertise to build complex systems and the creative judgment to use those systems effectively.

## Research & Professional Engagement

My professional interests include interactive simulation, immersive technologies, digital twins, AI-supported learning experiences, and the application of game development techniques beyond entertainment. I am particularly interested in how real-time interactive systems can support research, education, training, and collaboration across disciplines.

I engage with the broader computing, graphics, and game development communities through professional organizations and technical service, including:

- **IEEE:** Involvement in Digital Twins and Parallel Intelligence, the Operational Research Society of Singapore, and Women in Engineering.
- **ACM SIGGRAPH:** Professional engagement with computer graphics, digital art, and interactive technologies.
- **International Game Developers Association (IGDA):** Connection to the professional game development community.

## Education & Recognition

I hold a **Master of Education in Adult Education** and bring more than 20 years of experience in teaching and developing courses across game development, interactive media, immersive technologies, web development, and digital production.

My teaching and creative work have received recognition including the **Kermit E. Brown Teaching Excellence Award (2025)**, the **DaVinci Fellows Award for innovation in teaching**, and awards from the **American Advertising Federation**.

## Bringing Creative & Crazy Ideas to Life

I enjoy taking creative, sometimes crazy ideas and exploring how we can bring them to life through technology. Whether it’s developing a game, building a simulation, or creating an immersive experience, I love the challenge of turning *“What if?”* into *“What if we actually could?”*

For me, technology is a tool for expanding the boundaries of imagination. It’s about experimenting, solving problems, and finding ways to make seemingly impossible ideas possible. As one of my favorite Walt Disney quotes puts it, *“It's kind of fun to do the impossible.”* That spirit of creativity and exploration is at the heart of both my work and my teaching.
